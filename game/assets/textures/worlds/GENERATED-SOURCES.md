# Material sources — iteration 3

`wood.png` and `brick.png` were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-10-17 and copied into this project. They are base-color source tiles, not pre-rendered substitutes for 3D scenery. The original outputs remain in the tool's generated-image directory. Other tiles and derived material properties are authored by the reproducible world-material generator.

Both delivered PNGs are 1254 × 1254 pixels; the wood prompt requested 1024 × 1024. They are used at the delivered resolution. Seamless repetition was requested and inspected in game; mathematical edge matching is not guaranteed by the image generator.

## Wood prompt

Create a production game material base-color texture, 1024x1024 square. Seamlessly tileable hand-painted warm honey oak wood, for a colorful stylized 3D arcade racing game toy kitchen table and timber scenery. A continuous wood surface with long subtle organic grain running vertically, gentle natural color variation, tasteful soft painted craftsmanship, smooth fine grain at miniature scale. Flat neutral albedo ONLY: no baked light direction, no shadows, no specular highlights, no perspective, no objects, no border, no plank joints or nails, no lettering. Must tile cleanly on all four edges. Attractive polished storybook game-art style, restrained readable grain, medium warm golden tan rather than dark brown. The whole image is the material.

## Brick prompt

Create a production game material base-color texture, square seamless tile. Hand-painted terracotta brick masonry for a colorful polished storybook 3D arcade racing game, historic Dutch town Haarlem and park buildings. Warm varied muted orange-red handmade small bricks with soft rounded edges, subtle clay surface grain and a few tasteful wear marks, thin pale warm beige mortar, classic running bond with staggered horizontal courses. Attractive stylized material craftsmanship; clean controlled color detail, not photorealistic dirt. Orthographic straight-on flat neutral albedo only: no perspective, no cast shadows, no baked directional lighting, no specular highlights, no moss or plants, no objects, no borders, no text. Brick layout and all material must seamlessly repeat across ALL four edges. The whole image is usable material.
